02

The project page

One piece at a time, large. The project's details pinned on the left with a thumbnail of the next project waiting at the bottom; the pieces stream down the right, one per screen, at their own ratio. In the video this is what opens when you click a tile. It could be what "Reveal full project" opens.

03

The names index

A list, and one image. Project names in two columns at the bottom; one small image centred above that swaps as you hover each name. Could replace Things we make, or sit as the index before the wall.

04

The scatter

Thumbnails thrown across the page, then gathered. Pieces float at random positions and angles; on a scroll or a click they slide into a tidy grid. In the video this is the transition into the index. Press the button to see it again.

@SC@
05

The two-up

Two large pieces side by side, edge to edge. One row per project with a caption under it. The simplest of the five; reads like spreads.
