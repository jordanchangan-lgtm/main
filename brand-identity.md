# LEGACY — Brand Identity Guidelines

A premium car dealership based in Jordan. The brand stands for heritage, trust, and quiet luxury — cars that carry a story, sold by people who respect that story.

---

## 1. Brand Essence

- **Name:** Legacy
- **Category:** Premium / pre-owned & luxury automotive dealership
- **Location:** Jordan (Amman-based, regional reach)
- **Tagline options:**
  - "Drive the Legacy."
  - "Every car has a story."
  - "Heritage on wheels."
- **Brand personality:** confident, refined, timeless, understated, trustworthy
- **Audience:** affluent buyers 25–55, car enthusiasts, expats, professionals who value craftsmanship over flash

---

## 2. Logo System

Legacy uses a **dual logo system**: a wordmark for primary use and a symbol/monogram for compact applications (favicon, profile picture, embroidery, wheel-center stickers).

### 2.1 Wordmark
- Full word "LEGACY" in uppercase
- Custom-spaced serif with subtle modern cuts (high contrast between thick/thin strokes)
- Optional underline bar or thin horizontal rule below the wordmark
- Optional small tagline beneath in light sans-serif: "AUTOMOBILES — JORDAN"

### 2.2 Symbol / Monogram
- A stylised **"L"** integrated with a subtle road/horizon line or laurel arc
- Works inside a circle or shield for badge-style use
- Must be legible at 32×32 px (Instagram profile)

### 2.3 Clear Space
- Minimum clear space around the logo equals the height of the letter "L".

### 2.4 Minimum Sizes
- Wordmark: 24 px height digital / 15 mm print
- Symbol: 16 px digital / 8 mm print

### 2.5 Don'ts
- Don't stretch, skew, or rotate
- Don't recolor outside the approved palette
- Don't add drop shadows, bevels, or gradients to the mark
- Don't place on busy photo backgrounds without an overlay

## 3. Color Palette

A restrained, premium palette: deep neutrals + a single warm metallic accent.

### Primary
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Use |
|------|-----|-----|------|-----|
| **Obsidian Black** | `#0B0B0C` | 11, 11, 12 | 0/0/0/95 | Primary backgrounds, wordmark on light |
| **Bone White** | `#F5F1EA` | 245, 241, 234 | 2/3/7/0 | Backgrounds, wordmark on dark |

### Accent
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Use |
|------|-----|-----|------|-----|
| **Champagne Gold** | `#C9A961` | 201, 169, 97 | 22/33/72/3 | Symbol, highlights, dividers |

### Supporting
| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK | Use |
|------|-----|-----|------|-----|
| **Graphite** | `#2A2A2D` | 42, 42, 45 | 70/63/57/55 | Secondary text, UI |
| **Sand** | `#D9CFBE` | 217, 207, 190 | 14/16/27/0 | Subtle backgrounds, tints |
| **Signal Red** | `#8B1E1E` | 139, 30, 30 | 30/95/95/35 | Used sparingly for "SOLD" tags or alerts |

**Rule of thumb:** 70% neutrals (black + bone), 25% supporting (graphite/sand), 5% gold accent. Red is functional only — never decorative.

---

## 4. Typography

### Primary — Display / Logo
- **Font:** Cormorant Garamond (or Playfair Display as alternative)
- **Weight:** SemiBold / Bold
- **Tracking:** +120 (loose, for elegance)
- **Use:** Logo, hero headlines, car names on grid posts

### Secondary — Headlines
- **Font:** Montserrat
- **Weight:** Medium / SemiBold, ALL CAPS for short headlines
- **Tracking:** +50
- **Use:** Post titles, captions, price tags

### Body / UI
- **Font:** Inter (or Helvetica Neue)
- **Weight:** Regular / Medium
- **Use:** Specs, descriptions, web body copy

### Arabic Companion
- **Font:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic (matches Inter) and Noto Naskh Arabic for serif contexts
- Always pair Arabic with the same hierarchy as the Latin set

## 5. Visual Direction

### Photography
- **Mood:** cinematic, low-key, editorial
- **Lighting:** warm tungsten or golden-hour; controlled studio for showroom shots
- **Backgrounds:** matte black walls, polished concrete, desert / Wadi Rum exteriors, Amman cityscape at dusk
- **Composition:** three-quarter front, low angles for stance, tight crops on details (badge, stitching, wheel)
- **Color grade:** crushed blacks, warm midtones, slightly desaturated; gold/amber highlights

### Instagram Grid Direction
- **9-tile rotating template:**
  1. Hero car (full shot, dark background)
  2. Detail crop (badge / interior / wheel)
  3. Quote / tagline on bone or black with gold accent
  4. Spec card (car name, year, km, price)
  5. Hero car
  6. Detail crop
  7. Customer / showroom moment (B&W)
  8. Spec card
  9. Hero car
- Always alternate **dark / light / accent** tiles to create rhythm
- Keep gold to one element per tile maximum
- Use the symbol (not the wordmark) as a small watermark in the bottom-right corner

### Iconography
- Thin line icons, 1.5 px stroke, rounded caps
- Gold or graphite, never black on black

### Motion (Reels)
- Slow push-ins, parallax over still photos
- Type animates in letter-by-letter with the serif logo font
- Cinematic letterbox bars (top/bottom) optional
- Music: ambient cinematic / Arabic lo-fi instrumental

---

## 6. Voice & Tone

- **Voice:** confident, concise, well-read — the salesperson who doesn't need to sell hard
- **Tone:** warm but reserved; never hype, never excessive emojis
- **Languages:** English primary, Arabic equal-weight for local audience
- **Do say:** "A 2018 Range Rover Vogue — fully serviced, one owner, ready for its next chapter."
- **Don't say:** "🔥🔥 INSANE DEAL!! DM NOW 🔥🔥"

---

## 7. Applications

### Instagram Profile
- Profile picture: symbol on Obsidian Black background
- Bio: short, bilingual, ends with location pin + WhatsApp contact
- Highlight covers: gold line icons on black

### Business Cards
- 85 × 55 mm, matte black uncoated stock
- Gold foil wordmark on front
- Bone white reverse with details in Inter

### Showroom Signage
- Brushed brass + matte black panel
- Backlit wordmark at entrance

### Merchandise
- **T-shirts:** heavyweight black cotton, embroidered gold symbol on chest, large bone-white wordmark on back
- **Mugs:** matte black ceramic, gold symbol on one side, wordmark on opposite side
- **Key tags:** leather (cognac or black) with debossed wordmark

### Vehicle Branding
- Discreet decal on rear window: small gold symbol + "LEGACY — AMMAN" in Inter
