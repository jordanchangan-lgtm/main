05 — How it runs

Five steps,
one file.

One document set follows the vehicle from the specification sheet to the handover signature. Nothing is reconstructed after the fact.

STEP 01

Specification

The buyer sets make, model, year, trim and condition. Everything after this is measured against that sheet.

STEP 02

Sourcing

Units located against the specification and priced landed — not ex-yard — so the number quoted is the number paid.

STEP 03

Transport

Moved under our own transport licence, on transporters or under their own power, with the file travelling alongside.

STEP 04

Clearance

Titles, invoices and clearance documents assembled and checked before the vehicle reaches the crossing.

STEP 05

Handover

Delivered in Mosul or onward to the buyer, signed off against the same specification sheet it started on.

The distance is thewhole business.

Buying a vehicle is the easy half. Getting it to Mosul — intact, documented and on the day it was promised — is the half that has to be run properly.
