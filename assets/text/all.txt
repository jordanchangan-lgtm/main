MALLOUK GROUP

Owned by the Mallouk Family, Mallouk Group was established in Syria in
1981 with subsidiary companies and offices in Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq,
Egypt and China (Hong Kong, Shanghai & Jiangsu) .


The Mallouk Group is one of the pioneer business groups achieving
growth in several business sectors including Automotive Manufacturing,
Automotive Distributorship (branding, marketing, sales & after-service),
Public   Transportation,   Financing,   Medical   Equipment,   Property
Development & Constructions, Aviation and Education etc.


由马络克家族持有的马络克集团于1981年成立于叙利亚，并在约旦、
黎巴嫩、伊拉克、埃及和中国（香港、上海及江苏）设有分公司及办
事处。马洛克集团致力于汽车制造、汽车经销（品牌发展，市场营销、
销售及售后）、公共交通、金融、医疗器械、地产开发及建筑、航空
及教育等领域的商业发展并在各国市场取得了卓越成绩。
