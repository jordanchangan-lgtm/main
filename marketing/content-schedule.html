00

Market Brief — Why this works in Jordan

87%
of EVs cleared in Jordan are Chinese-made. The buyer already trusts the origin — we sell the brand & story, not the country.
0% sales tax
EVs are exempt from the 16% sales tax on petrol cars and sit on a low flat tax — pair the dream with the math.
450+
charging stations now cover the Desert Road, Dead Sea & Irbid routes every 70–100 km — the infrastructure objection is fading.
~1,000 km
total REEV range on a full tank + charge. This is the hero number for a country that drives Amman→Aqaba→Petra.
The strategic angle

Jordanians love the idea of an EV but fear being stranded on the way to Aqaba or up to the north. REEV kills that fear. Every Deepal post leans adventure + freedom (no fuel stops, go anywhere); every Avatr post leans premium + intelligence (silent luxury, Huawei tech, a statement in West Amman). Bilingual hooks, Jordanian dialect, evening posting.
