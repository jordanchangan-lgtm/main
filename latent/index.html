The method

No camera.
No studio.
Only direction.

No set build, no crew, no travel, no reshoot day. That is why the numbers further down look the way they do — and why a brand with one product and no budget for a shoot can still run a campaign that holds up next to one.

Categories where the product is small, the surface is everything, and a conventional shoot costs more than the launch it supports. Skin, glass, resin, fabric and metal are the five hardest things to fake — so they are the five we spend the most direction on.
