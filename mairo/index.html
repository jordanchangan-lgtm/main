Who we are

Three pieces. Nothing more.

Mairo is activewear reduced to three pieces: an asymmetric tank, a racerback bra and wide-leg trousers. Each is cut once, plainly, and made to be worn together.

What we make

Four colours. One sun.

The pieces come in four sets: red, cream, pink and purple. Each set is one colour family, shot in hard midday light against plaster, stone and water.

The point

Plain at the front. Always.

Nothing crosses, nothing creases, nothing is printed on the front. The name sits once, small, on the back. The colour does the rest.
