( Studio )

Latent Studio makes campaign imagery and product film for cosmetics, coffee, clothing, jewellery and perfume brands.

Nothing is photographed. Every frame is directed, generated, measured and corrected — and where a generator can't hold a product's geometry, the frame is composited from the real pack shot instead. The product stays the product.

The method

No camera.
No studio.
Only direction.

No set build, no crew, no travel, no reshoot day. That is why the numbers further down look the way they do — and why a brand with one product and no budget for a shoot can still run a campaign that holds up next to one.

Brand — Atelier Rebul Sector — Perfume Scope — Nine plates + film Format — 4:5 · 3712×4608 / 9:16 · 15.6s Status — Concept, not commissioned Year — MMXXVI

Élixir 2 opens on ripe plum, carries musk through its heart, and settles into vanilla and amber. Five notes. We made all five, plus the flower amber is actually harvested from, and the flacon that holds them.

No camera, no set, no crew. Nine 4:5 studies with type baked into the frame, and a fifteen-second vertical film cut to 165 BPM — eighteen cuts, the shortest holding 0.20s, the music dropping to −29 dB under the hero hold and stopping dead rather than fading.

Cubecare center

Brand — Cube Care Center Sector — Care Scope — Identity + campaign Status — Commissioned Year — 2026

Two pieces of work that had to agree with each other: the identity first — logo, palette, typography and the guidelines that hold them — then the campaign content built on top of it.

Because the frames are generated rather than shot, the identity isn't a document the photography drifts away from. The palette and type in the guidelines are the same ones directed into every carousel and every reel, so the system reads the same on the logo sheet and in the last frame of the feed.

Cubecoffee house

Brand — Cube Coffee House Sector — Coffee Scope — Identity + campaign Status — Commissioned Year — 2026

The same two-part build — identity, then the campaign frames that run on it — for a category where the product is almost entirely surface.

Coffee is one of the hardest things to direct without a camera: crema breaks in a particular way, steam has to read as hot rather than smoky, and roast colour shifts under every light you put on it. Those are the frames that get the most direction, and the ones that get cut when they don't hold.

Rawabinaal-khadhraa

Brand — Rawabina Al-Khadhraa · روابينا الخضراء Sector — Landscaping & irrigation Scope — Editorial website Region — Saudi Arabia, est. 1997 Status — Live

Touch of Green. A landscaping and irrigation contractor working on the Kingdom's landmark projects since 1997 — design, implementation and maintenance of premium landscapes.

An editorial build rather than a static one: the page is animated end to end, bilingual English and Arabic, set in Manrope and Tajawal, on a palette that runs from planting green through to irrigation blue. The argument the site makes is that landscape is a science — so the structure walks a visitor through listening, designing, building and maintaining, in that order.
