# AVATR 07 REEV (GCC) — Amman Showroom Marketing Strategy

**Market:** Amman, Jordan · **Showroom:** Mecca Street · **Car:** AVATR 07 REEV, GCC specs · **Price:** 42,000 JOD
**Core problems:** (1) Zero walk-ins despite prime location. (2) Meta boosting delivers audiences who can afford ~half the car's price. (3) Content is cinematic-only and doesn't convert the Jordanian buyer.

---

## 1. Why the boost button is failing you (diagnosis)

The "Boost" button is not broken — it's doing exactly what it's designed to do, and that design is wrong for a 42k JOD car:

1. **Boosting optimizes for engagement, not buyers.** Meta's algorithm hunts for the *cheapest possible* likes, comments, and views. In Jordan, the cheapest-to-reach users are young, high-scroll, low-income users. The algorithm will never "find" a 42k buyer when you pay it to find engagement.
2. **Boosted posts default to broad Advantage audiences and junk placements** (Audience Network, low-quality inventory) with almost no control.
3. **You're giving Meta no signal of who a real buyer is.** Without a pixel, lead-quality feedback, or customer lists, the algorithm learns from whoever engages — which is currently the wrong crowd, so it finds more of them. It's a feedback loop working against you.

**The fix is not a better boost — it's Ads Manager + a qualification funnel + feeding buyer signals back to Meta.**

## 2. New Meta ads architecture

### 2.1 Campaign objectives (stop using Engagement/Awareness for lead flow)

| Campaign | Objective | Role | Budget share |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 — WhatsApp Conversations | Messages (click-to-WhatsApp) | Primary lead engine — Jordanians buy over WhatsApp | 40% |
| C2 — Lead Forms (qualified) | Leads, "Conversion Leads" optimization | Structured, filtered leads for the CRM | 25% |
| C3 — Retargeting | Sales/Leads | Convert engagers & site visitors to test drives | 20% |
| C4 — Creative/audience testing | Varies | 2-week test cells (see §2.4) | 15% |
| (ongoing) Brand reels | Boost is acceptable ONLY here | Reach/frequency on warm Amman audiences for the cinematic layer | small |

### 2.2 Qualify inside the ad, not after it

- **Put the price in the creative.** "42,000 JOD" in the first 3 seconds is your cheapest targeting tool. Hiding the price attracts dreamers; showing it makes non-buyers scroll past, and Meta's algorithm then optimizes toward the people who *stayed*. Yes, reach drops. Lead quality is the goal, not reach.
- **Lead form qualifying questions (C2):**
  - "When are you planning to buy?" (This month / 1–3 months / Just looking)
  - "Budget range?" (Under 30k / 30–40k / 40k+ JOD)
  - "Cash or financing?" · "Do you have a trade-in?"
  - Use **Higher Intent** form type (review screen) + Conversion Leads optimization once CRM feedback is flowing.
- **WhatsApp ice-breakers (C1):** "احجز تجربة قيادة" / "شو سعر التقسيط؟" / "بدي تقييم سيارتي (Trade-in)" — routes intent instantly.

### 2.3 Audience stack (Jordan has no income targeting — use wealth proxies)

**Geo (most important lever):**
- Drop "Jordan" as a location. Target **pin-drops + 2–4 km radius** on affluent Amman districts: Abdoun, Dabouq, Deir Ghbar, Sweifieh, Um Uthaina, Khalda, Rabieh, Um Al-Summaq, Airport Road corridor, Fuheis.
- **Geofence Mecca Street itself + competitor showroom clusters** — people physically car-shopping this week.

**Demo:** 30–55, all genders (don't exclude women — family CFO effect is real in Jordan).

**Interest stacks (test cells, don't mush together):**
- *Luxury auto:* Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi, Lexus, Range Rover, Porsche + "Luxury goods"
- *Wealth behavior:* Frequent international travelers, business travel, real estate investing
- *EV-curious:* Electric vehicles, Tesla, BYD, hybrid vehicles (Jordan's EV import boom = large pool)

**Custom & lookalike audiences (build these week 1):**
- IG/FB engagers 365d, 75% video viewers, website visitors (install the Meta Pixel on the site — non-negotiable)
- Customer list: every past buyer + test-drive lead across all Mallouk brands → **1–3% Lookalike (Jordan)** — this will likely become your best prospecting audience
- Exclusions: existing leads/buyers from prospecting campaigns

### 2.4 Testing discipline (what "testing new things" should look like)

- One variable per test cell. Run cells 5–7 days minimum, ≥ ~10–15 JOD/day per ad set, then kill or scale.
- **Judge on cost per *qualified* lead and cost per test drive — never CPM, CPC, or engagement.**
- Test matrix for the first month:
  - Audience: Broad Amman-affluent-geo (creative does targeting) **vs** interest stacks **vs** lookalike
  - Creative: price-led hook vs REEV-range hook vs comparison hook
  - Format: salesman 1:1 vs cinematic vs hybrid (cinematic open → salesman close)
  - Destination: WhatsApp vs lead form

### 2.5 Close the loop (this is what permanently fixes audience quality)

1. Every lead tagged in CRM/sheet: *Qualified / Unqualified / Test drive / Sold*.
2. **Upload offline conversions (test drives, sales) back to Meta weekly.**
3. Switch C2 to Conversion Leads optimization once ~20+ qualified events/month flow. From then on Meta optimizes toward people who *behave like your real buyers*, not engagers. This is the structural cure for "everyone who responds can only afford half the car."
4. **Speed-to-lead: respond in under 5 minutes.** A 42k lead that waits 6 hours is a dead lead. Assign WhatsApp ownership per shift with templates.

## 3. Walk-in engine (making Mecca Street pay rent)

The showroom's job online is to be a *destination*, not a location.

1. **One CTA everywhere: "Book your test drive" (احجز تجربة قيادة)** — every reel, every ad, bio link, WhatsApp auto-reply. Confirmed slot, named salesman, car ready at the door.
2. **"Private viewing" framing** for the premium feel: 30-minute 1:1 appointment, coffee, no pressure. Costs nothing, converts the hesitant.
3. **Geofenced "already shopping" campaign:** radius ads around Mecca St + competitor showrooms: *"وأنت بتقارن سيارات اليوم… خمس دقايق عنا بتغير رأيك. AVATR 07 — تجربة قيادة فورية."*
4. **Monthly weekend event:** "REEV Road-Trip Day" — the Amman→Aqaba-and-back-without-charging story, live fuel-cost math, family-friendly, bank financing partner on site for instant pre-approval, trade-in valuation on the spot.
5. **Google layer (cheap, high intent, don't skip):** claim/optimize the Google Business Profile (photos, reviews from every buyer, WhatsApp link) + Search ads on "Avatr 07 سعر الاردن", "Avatr Jordan", "سيارات كهربائية فخمة الاردن". These searchers are *bottom-funnel*.

## 4. Content strategy — 3 reels/week, rich but Jordanian

Keep the cinematic ambition — it protects the 42k price point. But cinematic alone sells the *brand*, not the *car*. The weekly system:

### The 3-reel weekly framework

**Reel A — Cinematic Hero (brand & desire).**
One feature, one emotion, 20–30s, high-grade. But it must open with a *spoken or bold-text hook in the first 1.5s* — cinematic doesn't excuse a slow open. Shoot against Amman identity: Abdoun at night, Abdali skyline, Airport Road at golden hour, the showroom on Mecca St glowing at night. That's the "rich but ours" feeling.

**Reel B — Salesman 1:1 (trust & conversion).**
Your instinct here is right. Salesman talks to camera like he's talking to one customer, Jordanian dialect, demoing **one feature deeply** — not a full tour. Shot well (clean audio, stable, good light) so it stays premium. This format builds the human trust that closes 42k deals in Jordan.

**Reel C — Objection / Comparison / Hook reel (the shareable one).**
Directly answers what Ammanis actually say in the comments and the majlis. This is the reel that gets sent to the brother-in-law.

**Rhythm:** every reel ends with the same CTA (test drive / WhatsApp). Best-performing organic reels get promoted **using the existing post ID** so the ad carries the social proof (comments/likes).

### Hook bank (Jordan-tuned, premium tone)

1. "٤٢ ألف دينار. خليني أوريك وين راحوا." *(price transparency + interior tour — doubles as an audience filter)*
2. "من عمان للعقبة ورجعة… وبطاريتها ما خلصت." *(REEV kills charging anxiety — your single biggest advantage in Jordan)*
3. "أغلب الناس بتتردد تدفع هالمبلغ بسيارة صينية. هذا الفيديو إلهم." *(hit the objection head-on, then show the engineering: Huawei, CATL)*
4. "Huawei مش بس تلفونات." *(smart cockpit / driving-assist demo)*
5. "شو بتشتري بـ٤٢ ألف؟ ألمانية مستعملة ٢٠١٩… ولا هاي؟" *(new-Chinese-flagship vs used-German framing — the real cross-shop in Amman)*
6. "بنزينك بالشهر كم بيكلفك؟ احسبها معي." *(fuel-cost math on screen, REEV economics)*
7. "أهدى مقعد بالأردن." *(zero-gravity seat, massage — 'صالون فندق خمس نجوم')*
8. "زوجتك رح تختار هالسيارة قبلك." *(family/safety/space angle — the household decision-maker)*
9. "ليش وكالات مكة ستريت كلها بتراقب هالسيارة؟" *(location flex + curiosity)*
10. "أول ٣ ثواني بتسمع فيهم السيارة… ما رح تسمع إشي." *(cabin silence test with dB meter — demonstrable luxury)*
11. "جرّبها ٣٠ دقيقة. إذا ما تغيّر رأيك، القهوة علينا." *(test-drive CTA as a hook itself)*
12. "التكنولوجيا اللي بالسيارات الألمانية بـ٢٠٢٨… موجودة هون اليوم." *(tech-leapfrog positioning)*

### Production rules

- Hook (text + voice) inside 1.5 seconds. Subtitles on everything — most reels are watched muted.
- Jordanian dialect for B and C reels; A reels can be music-led with Arabic text overlays.
- Vertical-native 9:16, filmed for the phone, not crops of the TVC.
- One idea per reel. A reel that shows 10 features sells none.
- Recurring salesman = recurring face = trust. Make him a mini-celebrity of Mecca St.

## 5. Measurement — the weekly dashboard

Track weekly, in this order of importance:

1. **Test drives booked** (north-star) and cost per test drive
2. Qualified leads (budget 40k+ / buying ≤3 months) and cost per *qualified* lead
3. WhatsApp conversations started → % answered in <5 min → % booked
4. Showroom walk-ins (ask every walk-in "what brought you in?" — log it)
5. Reels: 3-second hold rate & average watch time (hook health), saves/shares (desire), *not* likes

Kill anything with a bad qualified-CPL after 7 days regardless of how cheap its CPM looks.

## 6. 30-day rollout

**Week 1 — Foundations:** Install Meta Pixel on the site. Build custom audiences + upload customer lists → lookalikes. Set up WhatsApp Business with templates + <5-min response ownership. Write qualifying lead form. Fix Google Business Profile. Brief the salesman-presenter and lock the 12-hook content calendar.

**Week 2 — Launch:** C1 (WhatsApp) + C2 (lead forms) live on 3 audience cells (affluent-geo broad / luxury-auto interests / lookalike). First week's 3 reels published. Retargeting audiences populating.

**Week 3 — Retarget & read:** Launch C3 retargeting with the test-drive offer. First kill/scale decisions on audience cells. Promote the best organic reel via post ID. Start logging lead quality per campaign.

**Week 4 — Optimize & event:** Shift budget to winning cells. First offline-conversion upload to Meta. Run the first weekend showroom event, geofence ads around Mecca St for it. Review: cost per test drive, walk-ins, and set month-2 budgets on data, not opinion.

---

## Summary of the three moves that matter most

1. **Kill the boost button for lead gen.** Ads Manager, WhatsApp + qualified lead forms, price shown in the creative, affluent-district geo targeting, and offline-conversion feedback so Meta learns what a real 42k buyer looks like.
2. **Make the showroom a destination:** one relentless CTA (book a test drive), geofence the street where people are already shopping, and a monthly event that gives Amman a reason to walk in.
3. **Keep cinematic as the frame, not the whole picture:** 3 reels/week = Cinematic Hero + Salesman 1:1 + Objection/Comparison, every one opening with a Jordanian hook in 1.5 seconds and closing with the same CTA. Rich and fancy, but speaking Ammani.
